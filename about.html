Our story

A Calgary mobile service built around downtime math

Every parked tractor burns money. JSB Truck and Trailer Repair grew from that simple fact: get certified people, stocked service trucks, and a Calgary dispatch that can reach Highway 1, Highway 2, and Stoney Trail without delay.

Who we are

We run mobile service units that meet owner-operators and fleet drivers wherever the unit stopped. The crew holds Red Seal credentials in commercial transport equipment and spends its weeks on Freightliner, Kenworth, Peterbilt, Volvo, International, and Cummins-powered iron.

The mission is straightforward: stop surprise downtime with honest diagnostics, OEM-grade or better parts, and emergency work that still respects safety and structure.

How we work

Four steps from call to road-ready

01

Find the fault

Computerized testing and a walk-around so we chase the real failure, not a related code.

02

Plan the repair

We map labour, parts, and how we will complete the job on-site at your unit.

03

Do the work

Service standards follow the truck: torque, safety, and structural rules do not get skipped on the shoulder.

04

Sign off

Functional checks and a clear work order before the unit is released back to dispatch.
